Why multimodal matters here

Customers describe products far better with a picture than with words.
Voice removes typing friction on mobile, where most traffic lives.
A single intent layer means no new journey to design per input type.

Boundaries that do not move

An uploaded image is used only to interpret this request.
Voice notes follow the same data-handling rules as text.
No modality can bypass a permission check or a confirmation step.
