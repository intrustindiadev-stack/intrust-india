Agentic commerce journey

From “I want this”
to a prepared checkout.

buying, without the maze

The commerce journey is where an agent earns its place — and where the boundaries matter most. The agent takes the customer to a review-ready cart; the platform keeps control of the money. Every step below maps to a capability InTrust already has today.

01 · ASK
Intent expressed

“I need running shoes under ₹3,000.”

02 · IDENTIFY
Product matched

Existing catalogue search and filters run.

03 · VALIDATE
Availability checked

Price, stock and size come from the platform.

04 · PREPARE
Cart assembled

The existing cart service adds the items.

05 · REVIEW
Customer confirms

Contents and total shown before anything is final.

06 · PAY
Secure checkout

Outside the agent — the platform completes the payment.

The agent's journey ends at step 05. Step 06 belongs to the platform's existing, audited payment flow.

What the agent may do

Find and compare products from the live catalogue.
Check availability, size and price before suggesting anything.
Add, update or remove items in the customer's own cart.
Summarise the cart and hand over to checkout.

What stays outside it

No card, wallet or UPI detail is ever seen or stored by the agent.
No payment authorisation, OTP or 3-D Secure step is triggered by the agent.
No order can be placed without the customer's own confirmation in checkout.
